1. Who We Are & Scope

Educational Times ("we", "our", or "the App") is an educator-led learning platform connecting teachers, contributors, and students with educational news, science innovations, articles, interactive quizzes, and polls. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use and safeguard it, and the privacy choices available to you.

Developer: Educational Times Editorial & Engineering Team
Privacy & Support Email: support@educationaltimes.org

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Registration Information

  • Identity & Contact: Full name, email address, and optional mobile number.
  • Age & Role Verification: Date of birth (used solely to classify Under 13, 13–17, or 18+ safety tiers), account type (Student, Teacher/Educator), class/standard (for students), school/institution, district, and state.
  • Optional Public Profile Details (18+ Users Only): Profile photo, short biography, academic designation, and optional website, LinkedIn, or X (Twitter) links you choose to publish or hide using in-app privacy toggles.

2.2 Google Sign-In Authentication

When you select Continue with Google, Android Credential Manager returns a cryptographic Google ID Token containing your Google email, display name, and profile avatar URL to authenticate your session with Supabase Auth. We never access or store your Google password, Gmail messages, contacts, or Drive files.

2.3 Educational Content & Interactions

  • Articles, drafts, attached images/PDF documents uploaded by approved adult contributors or the editorial Admin.
  • Comments (verified 18+ accounts only), likes, saved bookmarks, quiz attempts, poll votes, and safety reports submitted by users.
  • Aggregate Post Views: Opening a published post increments an anonymous aggregate counter (views_count). To prevent duplicate counting from rapid refreshes, the app saves a temporary 30-minute timestamp locally on your device using Android DataStore Preferences.

3. Children's Privacy & Under-18 Safety

Protecting minors is foundational to Educational Times. During sign-up (including Google Sign-In onboarding), every user undergoes server-validated date-of-birth age screening:

  • Verifiable Parental Consent: Users under 18 (including under 13) require parent or guardian details and consent confirmation.
  • Restricted Public Exposure: Under-18 student accounts cannot publish public articles, post public comments, apply as contributors, or exchange direct messages with adults.
  • Hidden Directory Fields: Institution names, districts, bios, and social links of minor accounts are excluded from public profile views.
Parents or guardians may review, update, or request immediate deletion of a child's account by contacting support@educationaltimes.org.

4. Device Permissions We Request

INTERNET ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional, Android 13+)

  • Network Access: Required to connect securely to our backend for educational content, authentication, quizzes, and polls.
  • Notifications (Optional): On Android 13+, requested only when opted in to deliver educational alerts via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). You can disable notifications anytime in App Settings or Android system settings.
  • Media Picker Privacy: Uploading a profile avatar or educational attachment uses the Android system Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia). We never request broad media or storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, or READ_MEDIA_VIDEO).

5. Advertising & Google Play Billing

  • Zero Ads for Students & Minors: Advertising (Google AdMob) is strictly disabled and never initialized for student accounts, users under 18, or accounts without verified adult status. When enabled for verified 18+ educator accounts, ads adhere strictly to a G-rated family-safe ceiling.
  • Contributor Subscriptions: Optional paid contributor memberships are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. Educational Times does not process or store your payment card details.

6. Service Providers & Data Security

We share data only with trusted infrastructure providers necessary to operate the app:

  • Supabase: Encrypted cloud PostgreSQL database, Row-Level Security (RLS), authentication, and media storage.
  • Google Play Services / Firebase: Native Google Sign-In authentication tokens, Firebase Cloud Messaging push transport, Play Billing, and adult-only AdMob delivery (when enabled).

All network communication is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Database access is governed by strict server-side Row-Level Security policies and trigger safeguards so users can access only authorized records.

7. Your Privacy Rights & Account Deletion

  • Access & Correction: Edit your profile details, visibility toggles (hide location or social links), notification preferences, or avatar photo anytime from the Profile tab.
  • Permanent Account Deletion: Open Profile → App Settings → Delete My Account inside the app to immediately submit an account and data deletion request, or email support@educationaltimes.org with the subject "Account Deletion Request - Educational Times".

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features, regulatory requirements, or safety enhancements. Material updates will be reflected by an updated Effective Date at the top of this page and inside the App's Privacy Policy section.
